# Statutory Sources & Documentation for Produce Quality & Grading Standards

This document outlines the official government statutory acts, regulatory authorities, operational manuals, and documentation links on which the **KrishiConnect Quality Assaying, Agmark Grading, and Mandi Procurement Standards** are modeled.

---

## 1. Department of Food & Public Distribution (DFPD), Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution, Govt. of India

* **Official Portal:** [https://dfpd.gov.in](https://dfpd.gov.in)
* **Quality Control Division:** [https://dfpd.gov.in/division/storage-research](https://dfpd.gov.in/division/storage-research)
* **Key Gazette Document:** *Uniform Specifications for Foodgrains for Kharif Marketing Season (KMS) & Rabi Marketing Season (RMS)*.

### Statutory Norms Applied in KrishiConnect:
* **Paddy (Dhan):**
  * Maximum Permissible Gate Moisture Limit: **17.0%**
  * Baseline FAQ Moisture: **14.0%** (entitled to 100% MSP payout without moisture deductions).
  * Foreign Matter (Inorganic + Organic): Maximum **2.0%** (Max 1.0% Inorganic).
  * Damaged, Discoloured, Sprouted & Weevilled Grains: Maximum **5.0%**.
  * Immature, Shrunken & Hollow Grains: Maximum **3.0%**.
* **Wheat (Gehun):**
  * Maximum Permissible Moisture Limit: **12.0%** (with statutory relaxation allowances up to 14.0% with proportionate value cut where officially notified).
  * Foreign Matter: Maximum **0.75%**.
  * Damaged Grains: Maximum **2.0%**.
  * Shrivelled & Broken Grains: Maximum **6.0%**.

## 2. Food Corporation of India (FCI)

* **Official Portal:** [https://fci.gov.in](https://fci.gov.in)
* **Procurement & Quality Control Guidelines:** [https://fci.gov.in/procurements.php](https://fci.gov.in/procurements.php)
* **Key Reference:** *FCI Quality Control Manual & Mandi Procurement Procedures for Central Pool Procurement*.

### Operational Protocols:
* Mandatory use of digital, calibrated moisture meters complying with **ISO 712 / BIS standards** at all procurement counters.
* Standardized value cut schedule for marginal quality variations within permissible statutory tolerance ranges.
* Official Form 'J' Weight Slip and Purchase Receipt generation upon intake verification.

## 3. Directorate of Marketing & Inspection (DMI), Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Govt. of India

* **Official Portal:** [https://dmi.gov.in](https://dmi.gov.in)
* **Agmarknet Portal:** [https://agmarknet.gov.in](https://agmarknet.gov.in)
* **Statutory Act:** *The Agricultural Produce (Grading and Marking) Act, 1937 (as amended in 1986)* and *General Grading and Marking Rules, 1988*.

### Agmark Grade Tiering Specifications:
* Formulates standards for over 220+ agricultural commodities spanning grains, pulses, oilseeds, and commercial crops:
  * **Mustard (Sarson):** FAQ Moisture $\le 8.0\%$, Upper tolerance $9.0\%$, Min $38.0\%$ oil content, 0% *Argemone mexicana* seed contamination.
  * **Jute (Pat):** FAQ Moisture $\le 18.0\%$, Permissible max $20.0\%$, TD-5/W-5 tensile strength grade, cleanly retted without root-cuttings.
  * **Maize (Makka):** FAQ Moisture $\le 14.0\%$, Permissible max $16.0\%$, Foreign matter $\le 1.5\%$, Damaged kernels $\le 3.0\%$.
  * **Potato & Onion:** Surface moisture $0.0\%$ (dry curing), defect cut limits $< 3.0\%$, size specifications (Potato $>45\text{ mm}$, Onion neck $<15\text{ mm}$).

## 4. Department of Food & Supplies, Government of West Bengal

* **Official Portal:** [https://food.wb.gov.in](https://food.wb.gov.in)
* **WB State Agricultural Marketing Board (WBAMB):** *West Bengal Agricultural Produce Marketing (Regulation) Act, 1972 (WAPM Act)*.
* **Key Circulars:** *Operational Guidelines for Decentralized Paddy Procurement at Centralized Procurement Centres (CPCs) & Direct Purchase Camps (DPCs)*.

### Mandi Courtyard Sun-Drying Grace Protocol:
* Mandates that farmers presenting produce with marginally high moisture (**$17.1\% - 19.9\%$**) are entitled to access the Mandi drying courtyard for a **2.5-hour free sun-drying grace window** before any official refusal or rejection action is registered.

## 5. Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) & Warehousing Development and Regulatory Authority (WDRA)

* **FSSAI Portal:** [https://www.fssai.gov.in](https://www.fssai.gov.in)
* **WDRA Portal:** [https://wdra.gov.in](https://wdra.gov.in)
* **Regulations:** *Food Safety and Standards (Contaminants, Toxins and Residues) Regulations* & *WDRA Scientific Grain Storage and Warehouse Rules*.

### Biological Spoilage & Safety Thresholds:
* Grain stored with moisture **$\ge 20.0\%$** undergoes rapid internal thermal heating and high-risk fungal colonization by ***Aspergillus flavus***, producing carcinogenic **aflatoxins**.
* KrishiConnect implements an automated **Safety Guard Block** at $\ge 20.0\%$ moisture to prevent spoiled lots from entering central silos and storage warehouses.

## Summary of Grading Logic Mapped to KrishiConnect

| KrishiConnect Grade Tier | Parameter Thresholds | Governing Standard & Payout Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grade A (FAQ Standard)** | Moisture $\le 14.0\%$, Chaff $\le 1.5\%$, Damaged $\le 2.0\%$ | 100% statutory MSP floor payout ([DFPD Uniform FAQ](https://dfpd.gov.in)) |
| **Grade B (Permissible Standard)** | Moisture $14.1\% - 17.0\%$, Chaff $\le 3.0\%$, Damaged $\le 4.0\%$ | 98% payout with 2% value cut adjustment ([FCI Procurement Rules](https://fci.gov.in)) |
| **Grade C (Sun-Drying Deferral)** | Moisture $17.1\% - 19.9\%$ | 2.5-hour mandi courtyard sun-drying grace window ([WBAMB / GoWB Guidelines](https://food.wb.gov.in)) |
| **Rejected (Spoilage Hazard)** | Moisture $\ge 20.0\%$ or heavy fungal rot | Intake blocked to protect central silos ([FSSAI & WDRA Storage Norms](https://www.fssai.gov.in)) |
